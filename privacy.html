Privacy Policy — Payslip Generator

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data stays on your device

Payslip Generator is a static website that runs entirely in your browser. Any content you enter (words, text, game state, invoice or payslip data) is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server. We have no database, no accounts, and no backend.

2. Local storage

The site may use your browser's localStorage to remember simple preferences and game statistics (e.g. win counts). This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared any time via your browser settings.

3. Third-party advertising (Google AdSense)

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve and personalise ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or aboutads.info for the broader industry opt-out. More detail is in Google's privacy policy for ads.

4. Analytics

We may use a privacy-friendly analytics script that assigns an anonymous visitor ID stored in your browser's localStorage. No personal identifiers are collected.

5. No cookies set by us

Payslip Generator itself does not set cookies. Cookies on this site are set only by third parties, primarily Google for advertising.

6. Contact

Questions? Contact Afro Walkman Studios via buymeacoffee.com/afrowalkmanstudios.

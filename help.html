How UK tax (PAYE) is calculated

  1. Enter the employee's gross monthly pay — before any tax or National Insurance.
  2. Enter their tax code (e.g. 1257L for the standard 2025/26 allowance). The last digit is ignored and the letters map to a code: L = basic allowance, M/N = marriage allowance adjustments, T = HMRC reviewed code.
  3. The tool annualises the allowance from the tax code, applies it monthly, and taxes the remainder at the basic rate (20%).
  4. If the salary pushes into the higher band, the amount above the higher-rate threshold is taxed at 40% (and above the additional threshold at 45%).

Example: gross £3,000/month, code 1257L → allowance £12,570/year = £1,047.50/month. Taxable £1,952.50 × 20% = £390.50 income tax for the month.

National Insurance

  1. Employee National Insurance is calculated on earnings above the Primary Threshold (PT).
  2. Main rate: 8% on earnings above the PT up to the Upper Earnings Limit, then 2% above that.
  3. Optional: tick pension to deduct a salary-sacrifice style pension percentage before tax is applied.

How Nigeria PAYE is calculated

  1. Enter the employee's gross monthly pay in Naira and switch the country to Nigeria.
  2. The tool applies the Personal Income Tax Act reliefs: Consolidated Relief Allowance = 20% of gross + ₦200,000, annualised.
  3. The remainder is taxed on the progressive 7%–24% bands for the tax year, applied monthly.

Print or save as PDF

  1. Fill in the fields and click Generate payslip.
  2. The payslip preview appears below the form.
  3. Click Print (or press Ctrl/Cmd+P).
  4. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF as the destination — the payslip is saved as a clean PDF, ready to send.

Troubleshooting

  • Tax looks too high/low — check the tax code and the period. A code like 1257L means a full year allowance; if the employee started mid-year the code is usually different (e.g. 1257L M1 or a K code).
  • Net pay is the same as gross — the person is below the tax and NI thresholds, which is correct for low earners.
  • Payslip prints on two pages — reduce the browser zoom before printing, or clear the notes area.
  • Wrong currency symbols — the currency follows the selected country; switch back if the format looks off.

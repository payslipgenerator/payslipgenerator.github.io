About Payslip Generator

Payslip Generator is a free, private payslip generator with UK and Nigeria PAYE tax logic, computed entirely in your browser.

It is a single-page website built by Afro Walkman Studios. Everything runs locally in your browser — your data never leaves your device, we have no accounts, no database and no backend.

The site is free and supported by advertising and the occasional coffee. If you find it useful, you can support it at buymeacoffee.com/afrowalkmanstudios.
